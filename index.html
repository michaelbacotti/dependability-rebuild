Initial Jobless Claims — 197K vs 188K revised prior

DOL weekly claims for the week ending July 25 rose to a seasonally adjusted 197,000, up 9,000 from the prior week's revised 188,000. The 4-week moving average fell to 202,750. The print came in cooler than the Kalshi market-implied 199,300 and well below the economist consensus range implied by recent weeks (~210-215K). Continued claims held at 1.78M (1.2% insured unemployment rate, unchanged). Labor market remains historically tight.
