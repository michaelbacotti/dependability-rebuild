Contact

Editorial contact

For corrections to published pieces, methodology questions, requests to expand on a specific concept, or general editorial inquiries, please email the editorial address listed in the site footer. We aim to respond within two business days. Verified corrections are appended to the bottom of the affected page with a dated correction note; minor corrections (typos, broken links) are made silently and noted in the next published post.

Partnership inquiries

For affiliate program inquiries, content partnerships, sponsored content proposals, and other commercial arrangements, please email the partnerships address with a brief description of the proposal. We do not currently accept sponsored content or paid placements on this site. We do participate in affiliate programs for tools and platforms we actually use; the affiliate relationships are disclosed on the relevant pages and do not influence the editorial content.

Press and media

Members of the press may reach out for comment, background interviews, or fact-checking. Please include your outlet, your deadline, and a brief description of the story. We respond to press inquiries within one business day when the deadline is within 48 hours. For longer-lead stories, we aim to respond within three business days. We do not provide pre-publication review of articles unless we are a named source.

Compliance and legal

DMCA notices, takedown requests, and other legal correspondence should be sent to the legal contact address with the subject line starting with "Legal:" so it is routed promptly. The publishing entity is Dependability Holdings LLC, a Nevada limited liability company. Service of process should be addressed to the registered agent in Nevada; the registered agent details are available from the Nevada Secretary of State.

Why no contact form

The site does not maintain a contact form because there is no backend to receive form submissions. All messages are routed through email. This is a deliberate choice: it eliminates the attack surface that comes with running a form-processing backend, and it makes the routing of different message types (editorial, partnership, press, legal) explicit and reliable. The email addresses are listed in the site footer.

For trading or investment questions, please consult a licensed professional — this site does not provide personalized advice. For technical issues with the site itself (broken pages, layout problems, accessibility issues), please email the editorial address with "site issue" in the subject line.

How We Respond

Reader emails reach our editorial inbox, not a generic info@ alias. We typically respond within 48 hours on business days; corrections requested with a source link are usually processed within 24 hours. If you're writing about a strategy question, we may ask clarifying questions about position sizing and underlying liquidity before responding — context matters more than the headline answer. We don't offer personalized investment advice and won't open a brokerage or advisory relationship over email; for that, see a licensed fiduciary.

Press inquiries: please use the dedicated press email (press@) with publication name and deadline. We don't do podcasts on the basis of cold pitches — read the site first, send a specific question with three sample article links, and we'll respond if it fits our editorial calendar. We will not appear on shows whose stated thesis conflicts with our published methodology.

About this article

Editor: The Dependability Holdings LLC Research Desk has tracked derivatives market structure and options positioning since the firm's launch in 2019, with a documented methodology and the “How we forecast” page that anchors every brief on the site.

Launched: Dependability went live in March 2024 as a single weekday morning brief covering S&P 500 levels, VIX dynamics, and what to do with options positions that week. Today it publishes a daily morning analysis and a weekly forecast. The journal of executed trades — every closed position with timestamp, brokerage reconciliation, and post-mortem — lives on tredey.com.

Editorial process: Each piece distils primary reporting (Cboe options data, OCC positioning, FRED macro series, SEC filings, Federal Reserve releases) into a worked-example frame: what the data says, why it matters, what to do this week. Forecasts are screened against the forecast archive and cross-checked against at least one confirming primary source before publication. Forecasts that turn out wrong receive a post-mortem within 30 days.

Corrections policy: When an article gets a fact wrong, we correct it inline and append a dated correction note at the top of the next morning brief. Send corrections to corrections@dependability.us — we aim to acknowledge within 48 hours.

Disclosure: Dependability Holdings LLC is a research entity, not a registered investment adviser or broker-dealer. Nothing on this site is investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security. The desk may hold positions mentioned in any article; the journal of closed trades is on tredey.com.
