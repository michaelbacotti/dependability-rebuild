MORNING BRIEF

VIX Tests the Cheap-Vol Floor; Equity Drift Extends Into Data-Light Tuesday

Overnight futures drift lower — ES 7,655 (-0.57%), NQ 29,225 (-0.98%); VIX expands +6.77% to 15.93 from Mon's 14.92 floor; WTI +2.67% to $88.05; calendar light ahead of Wed ISM.

Tuesday opens with the equity drift that defined Monday's positioning-confirmation tape extending into the pre-market session — S&P 500 e-mini futures sit at 7,655.25 (-0.57% from Monday's 7,699.00 settlement, per CME ES) and Nasdaq e-mini futures at 29,225 (-0.98% from 29,513.00, per CME NQ) in the 5:45 AM ET reference window. The session's defining offset is vol: VIX has expanded +6.77% from Monday's 14.92 close to 15.93 in pre-market trade (per Cboe VIX) — a meaningful re-pricing of the cheap-vol regime that had compressed to its lowest print since mid-July on Friday's Jackson Hole verdict. WTI crude continues the geopolitical-rebound sequence that began Friday, snapping another +2.67% overnight to $88.05 (per NYMEX CL=F) after Monday's +3.49% to $86.31. Today's US calendar is data-light — the next macro print is Wednesday September 2's August ISM Manufacturing PMI at 10:00 AM ET, the opening leg of the ISM / JOLTS / NFP arc that frames the September 16 FOMC.

Overnight Headlines

Overnight equity drift extends Monday's positioning-confirmation tape — ES 7,655.25 (-0.57%), NQ 29,225 (-0.98%) in 5:45 AM ET pre-market; tech underperforms larger caps as the AI-cohort cooling continues. Monday's session absorbed Friday's Jackson Hole verdict from Chair Warsh as a positioning-confirmation day rather than a fresh directional move: SPX -0.33% to 7,686.14 (per Cboe SPX), QQQ +0.05% to 716.76 (per Invesco QQQ), IWM -0.62% to 293.93. Tuesday's pre-market extends that drift with NQ leading the technology cohort lower by -0.98% — almost double the ES move — and YM at -0.62%, RTY -0.53%. The pre-market range for ES so far is 7,648.50 - 7,706.25, a tight 58-point band indicating low conviction in either direction ahead of the 9:30 AM ET cash open. The overnight tech underperformance (NQ -0.98% vs ES -0.57%) is consistent with the AI cohort continuing to absorb Friday's hawkish-Warsh verdict — the institutional framework's positioning holds that the AI capex re-acceleration thesis (structurally intact on NVDA Q2 FY27 actuals: $96.2B revenue, $108.0B Q3 outlook, $89.0B Data Center) is the dominant mega-cap theme, but the near-term tape is consolidating ahead of the AMD/AVGO/MRVL/MU earnings cluster in the next 2-3 weeks.

VIX expands +6.77% from Monday's 14.92 close to 15.93 in pre-market trade — the cheap-vol regime is being tested as the post-Warsh verdict absorption broadens into Tuesday's open. VIX closed at 14.92 Monday (from Friday's 14.43, +3.40%, per Cboe VIX) — no longer at the cheapest floor but still in the cheap-vol regime (1-year mean ~20). Tuesday's pre-market expansion to 15.93 is the canonical "positioning confirmation re-pricing" — the +1.01 absolute point move signals that the institutional options framework is treating the hawkish-Warsh verdict absorption as a more sustained regime feature than a one-day event. Per Cboe, VIX9D (9-day implied) sat at 12.34 Monday close while VIX3M sat at 17.53, giving a term slope of approximately -41% (front-month meaningfully below 3-month) — the deep contango that characterized the compressed-vol regime has softened as 9D catches up to VIX at the new 15.93 baseline. The structural read: the cheap-vol floor (Friday's 14.43) is no longer the operative reference — 15.50-16.00 is the new baseline heading into the ISM/JOLTS/NFP cluster. VVIX (vol-of-vol) closed Monday at 86.29 (+4.09% on the day), a level that historically signals vol expansion rather than compression ahead of macro catalysts.

Oil continues the geopolitical-rebound sequence — WTI +2.67% overnight to $88.05 (from Mon $85.76 settlement, per NYMEX CL=F); USO continues to track the inflation-fear-premium re-engagement. WTI crude futures extended Monday's +3.49% rally with another +2.67% overnight to $88.05, marking a +6.27% two-day cumulative move from Friday's $83.40 post-ceasefire low. The driver: ceasefire durability questions between Israel and Hamas continued through the weekend, with Iranian supply scenarios and broader Middle East risk re-emerging as market factors heading into the September calendar arc. USO (exchange-traded crude proxy) traded at $136.90 in pre-market (per USO Yahoo Finance) versus Monday's $133.70 close (+2.39% after-hours follow-through). The oil-spillover bid continues to flow into XLE — Monday's +2.04% to $63.96 (per SSGA XLE) was the cleanest sector read of the inflation-fear-premium re-engagement, and Tuesday's pre-market is consistent with energy maintaining leadership if the geopolitical risk premium holds. The structural read: a sustained WTI break above $88 area opens $90+ as the next reference; a failure to hold $86 would re-engage the post-ceasefire fade thesis.

Gold consolidates lower — GC $4,420.70 (-0.23% overnight from Mon $4,431.10); spot gold holding above the $4,500/oz structural floor but the hawkish-Warsh bid-break from Friday is consolidating into a $4,400-4,500 range. Gold futures gave back another -0.23% overnight to $4,420.70 (per COMEX GC=F), bringing the cumulative two-day pullback from Friday's hawkish-Warsh-driven selloff (GLD -3.24% to $408.89, spot gold $4,506/oz) to -0.49% net. The structural read: gold is finding a floor in the $4,400-4,500 area rather than continuing the Friday unwind — the $4,500/oz institutional framework reference is intact, but the structural real-asset bid has not yet reclaimed the prior $4,609/oz print. GVZ (Gold Vol) closed Monday at 24.40 (-8.96% on the day), one signal that the gold-vol regime is compressing even as VIX expands — a divergence that historically resolves in the gold-vol direction (i.e., gold-vol tends to catch up rather than VIX catching down).
