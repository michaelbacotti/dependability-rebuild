Wednesday's soft CPI absorption is the precedent — Thursday's PPI is the confirmation test. The S&P 500 closed Wednesday at 7,748.50 (+0.26% on the day, -0.06% WTD) as the July CPI print (headline +0.1% MoM / core +2.5% YoY; both down 0.1pp from June) cleared the path for a 25bp Fed cut at the September 15-16 FOMC. The test: if PPI confirms the CPI disinflation (core at or below +0.3% MoM), the post-CPI bid extends into next week. If core PPI surprises hot (+0.4%+ MoM), the September-cut probability repricing reverses and 10Y tests 4.80%.

Initial Jobless Claims at 8:30 AM ET — the labor-market pulse. Consensus 199K (versus prior 202K; 4-week moving average 198.75K). A sub-200K print supports the soft-landing thesis and a 25bp September cut; a >210K surprise would accelerate the cut path. The structural read: claims have stabilized near 200K for 4 weeks — the trend supports a "gradual cooling, not collapsing" framing for the September 15-16 FOMC.

Applied Materials (AMAT) reports Q3 FY2026 after the close — the eighth AI infrastructure validation bar. Consensus adjusted EPS $3.39, revenue $9.0B. AMAT closed at $548.15 (after-hours Wednesday) versus a 1-year mean of ~$420, fully priced for a beat. The risk is in the buy-side reaction: AMAT needs to either beat-and-raise (forward capex guidance) or beat-and-hold for the multiple to extend. A miss or in-line print would test whether the AI infrastructure cohort can hold the +1.49% XLK bid from Wednesday.

What to Watch at the Open

  • The 8:30 AM ET PPI print — the second binary event of the week. The consensus +0.2% headline MoM / +0.3% core MoM would confirm the post-CPI disinflation pipeline. The market is positioned for confirmation but not for a hot surprise: VIX 14.59, gold at $4,449.80 (+0.93% overnight), 10Y at 4.68% — all signal a soft-pipeline lean. The test: does the core MoM print at or below consensus, or does it surprise to the upside? A clean in-line print extends the soft-CPI bid into Friday and supports the 7,800 1-month target. A hot core print (+0.4%+ MoM) forces a 10Y retest of 4.80%, pressures TLT, and risks an SPX pullback to 7,700. Watch the 10Y-yield repricing within 30 minutes of the 8:30 release.
  • The 30-Year Auction at 1:00 PM ET — the duration test of the week. The 30Y is the cleanest read on the term-premium repricing after Tuesday's 3-year auction tail (4.291% vs 4.179% prior — a 12bp jump). A strong 30Y auction (tail <2bp) anchors the long end near 4.70% and supports the rate-sensitive cohort (XLRE +0.93% Wednesday, XLU +0.48%). A weak auction compounds any post-PPI hawkish repricing and pressures the duration-sensitive sectors — the cleanest way for the soft-PPI thesis to be tested.
  • WTI breaking $83 — does the Iran-Hormuz premium continue to compress, or does it defend? WTI slid -2.11% overnight to $81.51, breaking the 2-day defense of $83. XLE was effectively flat Wednesday (+0.16%) on the $83 defense, USO held $127.30 (-0.24%). The overnight oil drop did not lift equity futures meaningfully (+0.19% ES) — a divergence suggesting the equity market is treating the oil move as transient. The test: does WTI reclaim $83 by midday, or does it settle into the $80-82 range and force XLE lower? A persistent WTI/XLE divergence would signal the equity market is treating the geopolitical-premium compression as structural.

The Structural Read

Thursday is the second binary event of the week, and the setup is constructive-but-defensive. The Wednesday close at SPX 7,748.50 (+0.26% on the day, -0.06% WTD) was the textbook soft-CPI absorption: VIX compressed to 14.55, gold extended to $404.92 (+0.99%), XLK +1.49% on multiple-expansion, the rate-sensitive bid normalized (XLRE +0.93%, XLU +0.48%). The 10Y at 4.68% is the most important yield level since the 4.80% intraday high three weeks ago.

The structural posture is unchanged: long-term bullish on US equities, supported by earnings growth (+30% Q2), AI capex durability (TSMC's $60-64B raised 2026 capex guidance and $100B Arizona commitment, hyperscaler $300B+ aggregate), US manufacturing reshoring, healthcare innovation, and a Fed cut window opening if the disinflation pipeline holds. The PPI is the producer-side confirmation of Wednesday's CPI — a clean in-line print validates the cut window and supports the 1-month target of 7,800; a hot core print forces a 10Y retest of 4.80% and risks an SPX pullback to 7,650-7,700.

The institutional positioning overlay is unchanged from Wednesday's PM forecast: hedge funds are net short the S&P E-MINI (-1.29% OI), aggressively net short the Nasdaq-100 (-10.46% OI), heavy longs in gold (+53.19% OI) and the USD (+43.14% OI). The VIX term structure (^VIX9D 11.09, ^VIX 14.59, ^VVIX 88.50, slope -23.99%) confirms protection is cheap. The next catalyst that resolves this asymmetry is the September 15-16 FOMC SEP — the cleanest gate to the 1-month target of 7,800.

Bottom line: Thursday opens with futures modestly bid (ES +0.19%, NQ +0.10%, RTY +0.22%), WTI sharply lower (-2.11% to $81.51), gold firmer (+0.93% to $4,449.80), 10Y holding 4.68%. The 8:30 AM ET July PPI print (consensus +0.2% headline MoM, +0.3% core MoM) is the second binary event of the week — a clean in-line print extends the soft-CPI bid and supports the 7,800 target; a hot core print forces a 10Y retest of 4.80% and an SPX pullback to 7,650-7,700. The 30-year auction at 1:00 PM ET and AMAT earnings after close round out the session.
