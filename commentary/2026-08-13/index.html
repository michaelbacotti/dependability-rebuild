Morning Market Analysis — Thursday, August 13, 2026

Thursday opens with one event defining the session: the July PPI report at 8:30 AM ET, the producer-side confirmation of Wednesday's soft July CPI print. The market is positioned defensively into the print — S&P 500 futures (ES=F) at 7,785.00 (+0.19% versus Wednesday's 7,770.50 settle), Nasdaq (NQ=F) at 29,882.25 (+0.10%), Russell 2000 (RTY=F) at 3,059.60 (+0.22%) — but the overnight tape delivered a sharp WTI drop to $81.51 (-2.11% from $83.27) that broke the Iran-Hormuz risk premium. Gold extended to $4,449.80 (+0.93%), 10Y at 4.68%, VIX 14.59. Consensus: headline PPI +0.2% MoM (vs June -0.3%), core PPI +0.3% MoM, core PPI YoY +4.2% (vs +4.7%). A clean in-line print confirms the disinflation pipeline; a hot core print forces a 10Y retest of 4.80% and pressures the duration bid.

Overnight Headlines

Overnight action: WTI breaks $83, gold extends, futures drift higher — the Iran-Hormuz premium unwinds. WTI crude (CL=F) is at $81.51 in the overnight session, down -2.11% from Wednesday's $83.27 settle — the most decisive overnight energy move of the week. Gold (GC=F) extended to $4,449.80 (+0.93% from $4,408.90), the safe-haven bid intact despite equity-futures strength. S&P 500 futures drifted +0.19% to 7,785.00, Nasdaq +0.10%, Russell 2000 +0.22% — a measured risk-on tilt that does not yet signal conviction ahead of the 8:30 AM ET print. The structural read: equities and bonds did not move much on the energy drop — the market is treating the move as idiosyncratic, not as a demand-destruction warning.

Wednesday's soft CPI absorption is the precedent — Thursday's PPI is the confirmation test. The S&P 500 closed Wednesday at 7,748.50 (+0.26% on the day, -0.06% WTD) as the July CPI print (headline +0.1% MoM / core +2.5% YoY; both down 0.1pp from June) cleared the path for a 25bp Fed cut at the September 15-16 FOMC. The cross-asset response was textbook soft-CPI absorption: VIX compressed to 14.55, TLT held $82.11, gold extended to $404.92 (+0.99%), XLK +1.49%. The test: if PPI confirms the CPI disinflation (core at or below +0.3% MoM), the post-CPI bid extends into next week. If core PPI surprises hot (+0.4%+ MoM), the September-cut probability repricing reverses and 10Y tests 4.80%.

Initial Jobless Claims at 8:30 AM ET — the labor-market pulse. Consensus 199K (versus prior 202K; 4-week moving average 198.75K). A sub-200K print supports the soft-landing thesis and a 25bp September cut; a >210K surprise would accelerate the cut path. The structural read: claims have stabilized near 200K for 4 weeks — the trend supports a "gradual cooling, not collapsing" framing for the September 15-16 FOMC.

Applied Materials (AMAT) reports Q3 FY2026 after the close — the eighth AI infrastructure validation bar. Consensus adjusted EPS $3.39, revenue $9.0B. AMAT closed at $548.15 (after-hours Wednesday) versus a 1-year mean of ~$420, fully priced for a beat. The risk is in the buy-side reaction: AMAT needs to either beat-and-raise (forward capex guidance) or beat-and-hold for the multiple to extend. A miss or in-line print would test whether the AI infrastructure cohort can hold the +1.49% XLK bid from Wednesday.

What to Watch at the Open

  • The 8:30 AM ET PPI print — the second binary event of the week. The consensus +0.2% headline MoM / +0.3% core MoM would confirm the post-CPI disinflation pipeline. The market is positioned for confirmation but not for a hot surprise: VIX 14.59, gold at $4,449.80 (+0.93% overnight), 10Y at 4.68% — all signal a soft-pipeline lean. The test: does the core MoM print at or below consensus, or does it surprise to the upside? A clean in-line print extends the soft-CPI bid into Friday and supports the 7,800 1-month target. A hot core print (+0.4%+ MoM) forces a 10Y retest of 4.80%, pressures TLT, and risks an SPX pullback to 7,700. Watch the 10Y-yield repricing within 30 minutes of the 8:30 release.

The test: does WTI reclaim $83 by midday, or does it settle into the $80-82 range and force XLE lower? A persistent WTI/XLE divergence would signal the equity market is treating the geopolitical-premium compression as structural.

The Structural Read

Thursday is the second binary event of the week, and the setup is constructive-but-defensive. The Wednesday close at SPX 7,748.50 (+0.26% on the day, -0.06% WTD) was the textbook soft-CPI absorption: VIX compressed to 14.55, gold extended to $404.92 (+0.99%), XLK +1.49% on multiple-expansion, the rate-sensitive bid normalized (XLRE +0.93%, XLU +0.48%). The 10Y at 4.68% is the most important yield level since the 4.80% intraday high three weeks ago.

The structural posture is unchanged: long-term bullish on US equities, supported by earnings growth (+30% Q2), AI capex durability (TSMC's $60-64B raised 2026 capex guidance and $100B Arizona commitment, hyperscaler $300B+ aggregate), US manufacturing reshoring, healthcare innovation, and a Fed cut window opening if the disinflation pipeline holds. The PPI is the producer-side confirmation of Wednesday's CPI — a clean in-line print validates the cut window and supports the 1-month target of 7,800; a hot core print forces a 10Y retest of 4.80% and risks an SPX pullback to 7,650-7,700.

The institutional positioning overlay is unchanged from Wednesday's PM forecast: hedge funds are net short the S&P E-MINI (-1.29% OI), aggressively net short the Nasdaq-100 (-10.46% OI), heavy longs in gold (+53.19% OI) and the USD (+43.14% OI). The next catalyst that resolves this asymmetry is the September 15-16 FOMC SEP — the cleanest gate to the 1-month target of 7,800.
