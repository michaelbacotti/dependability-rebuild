Overnight Headlines

AMAT sold off into the AI infrastructure cohort's biggest day of the week. Tapestry (TPR) closed Thursday -16.49% at $128.39 (from a $153.74 prior close) — the consumer-discretionary multiple-derating event. TPR reported FY2026 Q4 before the open: Q4 beat consensus (adjusted EPS $1.32 vs $1.25–$1.28, revenue $1.876B vs ~$1.86B), but FY27 revenue guidance of $8.4–$8.5B (midpoint $8.45B) was 0.1–0.2% below consensus; FY27 adjusted EPS guidance of $7.80–$7.90 (midpoint $7.85) was essentially in line. The selloff was a multiple reset, not an earnings cut: revenue growth slows from +14% to ~+5.8% comparable, Kate Spade is guided to a second straight high-single-digit decline, and Coach is now expected to carry more than 100% of net growth. The structural read: TPR is a single-stock derating event, not a consumer-broad signal — XLY closed +0.48% on Thursday.

Thursday's record close at 7,798.99 (+0.65%) was the cleanest cool-PPI absorption day of the week. The July Producer Price Index came in at 0.0% MoM headline (vs +0.2% consensus, June -0.1% revised), annual headline 4.7% — the slowest pace since late 2024. The cross-asset response was textbook: 10Y drifted to 4.64% (-4 bp day, -6 bp WTD), VIX held 14.63, TLT +0.58%, gold gave back -1.47%, energy led lower (USO -1.78%, XLE +0.05%) on the goods-deflation leg. CME FedWatch probability of a September 25 bp cut rose to 65% (from ~42% pre-CPI). The core PPI complication: core PPI accelerated to +0.4% MoM (vs +0.3% consensus) on a 6.5% portfolio management fee surge (a volatile, equity-correlated category). The market absorbed the cool headline as dominant; the hot core was noted but not dominant. The structural read: the CPI/PPI pair has confirmed the disinflation pipeline (CPI +0.1% MoM, PPI 0.0% MoM — both below consensus). The path to a 25 bp cut at the September 15-16 FOMC is firmly live.

The institutional positioning overlay is the asymmetry most worth monitoring.
