Morning Market Analysis — Wednesday, August 12, 2026

The tape is opening Wednesday with one event defined above all others: the July CPI report at 8:30 AM ET — the dominant binary catalyst of the week. Pre-market positioning is constructive but not euphoric: S&P 500 futures (ES=F) at 7,766.00 (+0.24% versus Tuesday's 7,747.50 settle), Nasdaq (NQ=F) at 29,817.75 (+0.65%), Russell 2000 (RTY=F) at 3,038.20 (+0.09%) — a futures-led bid that suggests positioning for a soft print. The 10-year yield held at 4.68% Tuesday (down from Monday's 4.70%, a 2bp relief rally), the VIX eased to 15.28, and gold extended to GLD $404.78 pre-market (+0.95% from Tuesday's $400.96). WTI is steady at $83.45. Consensus core CPI +0.2% MoM / +2.5% YoY and headline +0.1% MoM / +3.4% YoY (vs June -0.4% MoM / +3.5% YoY) — a clean in-line print confirms the disinflation trend, opens the door to a September Fed cut, and supports the 1-month target of 7,800. A hot core print (+0.3%+ MoM or +2.6%+ YoY) forces a 10Y retest of 4.80% and risks a SPX pullback to 7,650-7,700.

Overnight Headlines

Tuesday's Bond Rally Sets the Stage — 10Y Eased 2bp to 4.68%, VIX Calmed to 15.28. The 10-year yield (^TNX) closed Tuesday at 4.68%, down 2bp from Monday's 4.70% — a modest relief rally heading into the CPI binary. TLT closed at $82.19 (+0.16% vs Monday's $82.06). The VIX closed at 15.28 (yfinance), down from Monday's 15.46 — the options market is not pricing meaningful alarm (implied move sub-1% for an SPX session). The 2bp relief is not a wholesale dovish repricing: the 3-year note auction cleared at 4.291% vs 4.179% prior (a 12bp jump), suggesting the front end is carrying more inflation premium than the long end. The 10Y at 4.68% is the most important yield level since the 4.80% intraday high three weeks ago.

Pre-Market Futures Bid — ES +0.24%, NQ +0.65% — Constructive, Not Euphoric. S&P 500 futures (ES=F) at 7,766.00 (+0.24% vs Tuesday's 7,747.50 settle), Nasdaq (NQ=F) at 29,817.75 (+0.65%), and Russell 2000 (RTY=F) at 3,038.20 (+0.09%) — a tech-tilted bid suggesting positioning for a soft CPI print. XLK is +0.82% pre-market at $187.62 (public.com) versus Tuesday's $186.09, and QQQ is +0.62% at $722.90 — the AI infrastructure names are catching the bid. XLE is -0.27% pre-market at $60.77 (public.com) despite WTI holding at $83.45, suggesting the commodity bid is fading while the equity energy complex consolidates. Gold (GLD) is +0.95% pre-market at $404.78 (public.com), extending Tuesday's defense of $400. The setup: futures up, bonds steadier, gold firmer — a clean pre-CPI risk-on tilt that could reverse sharply on a hot print.

Energy and the Iran-Hormuz Premium — WTI Holding $83.45, XLE Consolidating. WTI crude (CL=F) is at $83.45 in the pre-market, up 0.30% from Tuesday's $83.20 settle, holding the 2-session Iran-Hormuz risk premium. USO is +0.56% pre-market at $127.20 (public.com) vs Tuesday's $126.50. XLE is -0.27% pre-market at $60.77 (public.com) — a divergence that signals the equity market is not extending the geopolitical-risk bid. The API crude oil inventory report Tuesday evening showed a +9.072M barrel build (vs +2.69M prior, consensus -0.50M expected) — a large bearish inventory surprise that has yet to weigh on the pre-market crude price. The structural read: oil's $83 floor is the most important commodity level of the week. A confirmed Hormuz deal would compress WTI toward $75 and weigh on XLE; absent a deal-clarification catalyst, the risk premium stays.

A soft print: 7,800 activates. A hot print: 7,650-7,700 pullback, 10Y retests 4.80%.

The market is positioned for the soft print. The futures bid is the cleanest signal. The structural bullish case has not been broken by Tuesday's -0.32% session. The disinflation trajectory is intact.
