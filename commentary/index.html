Morning Update — Overnight Bounce After Hawkish-Dissent FOMC; AMZN Q2 Caps the Week

ES +1.09%, NQ +1.05%, RTY +0.79% overnight after Wed's -1.52% post-FOMC selloff. WTI $83.52, gold +2.5%, VIX 20.66. Q2 GDP + claims 8:30 AM; AMZN Q2 after-close.

Morning Market Analysis — Thursday, July 30, 2026

The market's post-FOMC hawkish-dissent selloff is being modestly bought overnight, with S&P 500 futures (ES) up +1.09% to 7,396, Nasdaq futures (NQ) up +1.05% to 27,707, and Russell 2000 (RTY) up +0.79% at 2,929.20 as traders digest the Warsh Fed's 9-3 hold at 3.50-3.75% with three hawkish dissents recorded Wednesday. The VIX spike to 20.66 — up +13.5% from Tuesday's 18.21 — landed squarely in the range the yesterday afternoon forecast had estimated (~19.5-21 area), confirming the post-meeting vol regime has reset higher without panic. The day's catalysts are stacked: Q2 GDP Advance Estimate and Weekly Jobless Claims at 8:30 AM ET frame the macro read, then Amazon Q2 earnings after the close (consensus EPS $1.82 on $196.18B revenue per AlphaStreet) is the structural test for AI capex durability into 2H 2026.

Overnight Headlines

Overnight Bounce Recovers Wednesday's Post-FOMC Selloff — ES +1.09%, NQ +1.05%, RTY +0.79%: Markets are stabilizing after Wednesday's -1.52% SPX selloff, the steepest single-day drop since the late-June Iran escalation. ES at 7,396 is +79.85 above Wednesday's 7,316.15 close; NQ at 27,707 implies QQQ ~$667.50 vs Wednesday's $660.94 (+1.00%); RTY at 2,929.20 implies IWM ~$292.92 vs Wednesday's $287.83 (+1.77%). The pattern is constructive — a gap higher, not a sharp reversal — suggesting the post-FOMC steepener is being absorbed at the 7,316 level. The 30Y held at 5.193% Wednesday and the 10Y at 4.62%, signaling that the bond market has found a clearing level after the steepener.

Oil Pulls Back to $83.52 (-1.11%) as Iran Premium Cools; Gold Surges +2.50% Overnight: WTI crude settled at $83.52 in early European trade, down from Wednesday's $84.46 settle (-1.11%) — modest cooling of the Iran premium that pushed WTI from Tuesday's $79.26 close to Wednesday's $84.46 area. The Strait of Hormuz remains effectively closed (Day 152; ~10 ships last week vs ~88/day normal), but the market is pricing de-escalation rhetoric (Trump diplomatic engagement via China, Pakistan) more than the active threat. Gold is the standout overnight mover: GC=F at $4,135.60, up +2.50% from Wednesday's $4,034.70 settle — the largest single-session gold move in weeks and a clear risk-off bid. GLD prints at $373.95; the gold surge alongside a flat dollar (DXY 100.75, -0.05%) suggests genuine safe-haven demand.

VIX at 20.66 Confirms Hawkish-Dissent Vol Regime: The +13.5% single-session VIX spike from Tuesday's 18.21 close is the cleanest post-FOMC vol regime the market has printed under the Warsh Fed: not panic-level, not benign, but a "hawkish-dissent holding pattern" that keeps bid/ask spreads wide and protective put pricing elevated. A 10% OTM put on SPY (the ~$656 strike for 90-day expiry, SPY $729.46, implied SPX ~7,295) now costs roughly 1.20-1.30% of notional — up from 1.05-1.10% pre-FOMC but below the 1-year mean of ~1.40%. AMZN Q2 after-close is the next binary vol catalyst.

AMZN Q2 Anchors the Day — Consensus EPS $1.82 / $196.18B Revenue / AWS Growth the Read: Amazon reports Q2 after the close (consensus EPS $1.82 on $196.18B revenue, +17.0% YoY; EPS range $1.56-$2.10; 46 analysts per AlphaStreet). The structural reads: AWS growth (~+18-28% YoY per TheStreet raised estimates) and capex guidance ($200B 2026 baseline; rumored raise would re-anchor AI infrastructure durability). After Alphabet raised 2026 capex to $195-205B last week, AMZN's guidance is the test for whether the AI capex cycle is broadening or narrowing.

  • (Friday Jul 31 — Jul PCE 8:30 AM ET): Consensus +0.2% MoM core, +2.6% YoY — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge; dominant catalyst for September cut probability.

What to Watch at the Open

  1. ES +1.09% vs. VIX at 20.66 — does the gap-up extend or fade at the bell? The +13.5% VIX spike signals the post-FOMC vol regime is not fully absorbed. If ES holds above 7,400 into the cash open, the gap is a structural re-anchor; if ES fades back toward 7,370 in the 9:30-10:00 AM ET window, the gap was short-covering and the tape is still post-FOMC risk-off. A clean break above 7,400 re-activates the 7,500 target path; a fail at 7,370 keeps consolidation in play into AMZN after-close.
  1. Q2 GDP + Jobless Claims 8:30 AM — the soft-landing re-confirmation test. Atlanta Fed GDPNow's tracking of Q2 GDP at +1.7% is materially below the +2.5% consensus; a +2.5%+ print would force the model to revise higher and support rate-sensitive cyclicals at the open. A sub-+2.0% print would amplify post-FOMC selloff pressure and reinforce the bond market's cautious pricing. Claims above 240K reinforce labor softening and support September-cut probability; below 230K is a hawkish surprise.
  1. AMZN Q2 setup — is the capex narrative broadening or narrowing? The chip-sector rout (NVDA -1.11% pre-market Tue, ASML -7% Monday, CXMT +530% IPO surge per the Tuesday morning brief) reflects the market pricing AI capex moderation. AMZN's Q2 print is the test: a capex raise to $130B+ for 2H 2026 with AWS growth at +20% YoY re-anchors the multi-year AI capex durability thesis. A capex hold or trim with AWS deceleration extends the chip rout and pressures XLK, QQQ, NVDA at Thursday's close and into Friday's tape. Consensus EPS $1.82 / Revenue $196.18B is the base case; the read-through to Alphabet's $195-205B capex raise is the structural signal.

The Structural Read

The interaction between Wednesday's hawkish-dissent FOMC and Thursday's overnight bounce sets up a structurally interesting tape. The bond market absorbed the 30Y +9 bp spike to 5.193% and the 10Y held at 4.62%, suggesting the Warsh Fed's 9-3 hold was fully priced by yesterday's close. The equity market is testing whether the steepener extends or stabilizes; the overnight ES +1.09% gap is a constructive signal that buyers are stepping in at 7,316. The VIX at 20.66 (vs 18.21 pre-FOMC) is the cleanest gauge: elevated, consistent with a market that needs another data point to confirm direction.

The structural bull case remains intact: disinflation is the dominant trend; the AI capex cycle is multi-year (pending AMZN's confirmation today); the Q2 bank earnings cycle validated the financial-sector thesis. The hawkish-dissent repricing is event-driven, not thesis-breaking. The path of least resistance is a consolidation in the 7,300-7,400 range into AMZN Q2 after-close and Friday's Jul PCE print — the consensus year-end 7,800 target is +6.62% above today's 7,316.15. For energy, the WTI pullback from $84.46 to $83.52 is modest cooling of the Iran premium but not a regime change; the gold surge +2.50% overnight is the strongest signal that the safe-haven complex is pricing genuine geopolitical + policy uncertainty.

Bottom line: The overnight bounce recovers Wednesday's post-FOMC selloff and signals buyers are stepping in at 7,316. Today's binary catalysts are Q2 GDP + Jobless Claims at 8:30 AM (the soft-landing re-confirmation test) and AMZN Q2 after-close (the AI capex durability test). A constructive print pair (GDP ≥+2.5%, claims ≤235K, AMZN beat + capex raise) would re-activate the 7,500+ path; a weak print pair (GDP sub-+2.0%, claims ≥245K, AMZN capex trim) would re-test the 7,250-7,300 support band. Smart positioning is to stay allocated through the data, use AMZN after-close as the structural confirmation signal, and view Friday's Jul PCE as the next binary event.
