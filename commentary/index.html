Morning Update — AMZN Q2 Blowout, AAPL −7% Pre-Market; PCE Caps the Week

AMZN +12% premarket on AWS +37% YoY (fastest since 2021); AAPL −7% on weak Q3 guide. SPX 7,437.63 Thu. PCE 8:30 AM ET is the binary catalyst.

Morning Market Analysis — Friday, July 31, 2026

The Q2 earnings tape is sorting AI winners from losers with brutal clarity: Amazon (AMZN) is +12% in premarket trading (~$263) following Thursday's after-close Q2 print that landed the strongest AWS expansion since 2021, while Apple (AAPL) is −7% premarket on weak Q3 guidance citing memory-supply constraints. Thursday's MSFT +15.5% (record one-day value gain in MSFT history per Motley Fool Midday Update verified) and AMZN's +12% premarket have ended the debate over whether the AI capex cycle is real — the question now is which companies can fund the capex without breaking free cash flow. ES +0.36% to 7,499.25, NQ +1.13% to 28,556.50, RTY +0.39% at 2,965.90, and the VIX normalized to 16.97 (-17.9% from the post-FOMC spike peak of 20.66). Today's binary macro print: June PCE Inflation Report at 8:30 AM ET — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge and the cleanest read on whether the post-FOMC re-rate extends into the close.

Overnight Headlines

AMZN +12% Premarket After Q2 Print — Revenue $200.6B, AWS +37% YoY, Op Income $27.5B (+43% YoY): Thursday after the close, Amazon reported Q2 revenue $200.61B vs $196.47B expected (+20% YoY per LSEG), AWS revenue +37% year-over-year — fastest growth since 2021, operating income $27.5B (+43% YoY), $169B annualized AWS run rate. Capex guidance raised to $220B for 2026, AWS backlog hit $496B. AMZN premarket $262.98 (public.com) implies +11.6% vs Thursday's $235.50 close — the cleanest signal yet that AI capex durability is being repriced upward.

AAPL −7% Premarket on Weak Q3 Guide — The Counterpoint Trade: AAPL prints $308.15 premarket (public.com), -7.6% from Thursday's $333.43 close (yfinance verified), after issuing Q3 revenue guidance of 9-11% YoY growth — below consensus — citing memory-supply constraints. The tape is sorting: companies with capex generating measurable revenue growth are rewarded (AMZN +12%, MSFT +15.5%); capex-constrained companies are penalized (AAPL −7%). META sits in the middle after Wednesday's beat-and-miss (revenue beat, EPS missed 14% on capex load, per MarketBeat, 24/7 Wall St. verified). The structural read: AI capex thesis is broadening into the supplier ecosystem (NVDA, AVGO, custom silicon) but narrowing for capex-loaded end-users with weaker monetization.

WTI +1.56% to $84.94, VIX Normalizes to 16.97, Gold Flat at $4,111: WTI up +1.56% from Thursday's $83.59 settle to $84.94 pre-market (yfinance CL=F verified), likely a function of risk-on demand plus modest Iran premium per the Strait of Hormuz Day 152 framework. Gold is essentially flat (+0.28% to $4,111.70). The VIX dropped to 16.97 (-0.70% from Thursday's 17.09), well below the post-FOMC spike peak of 20.66 — a -17.9% drawdown in 48 hours. The vol regime that spiked on the Warsh-Fed-hawkish-dissent repricing has fully normalized. The 10Y yield held at 4.663% Thursday close (^TNX, +4.1 bp from Wed's 4.622%) — steepener arrested but did not reverse.

June PCE Inflation Report 8:30 AM ET — The Fed's Preferred Gauge Caps the Week: The Bureau of Economic Analysis releases the June PCE Inflation Report at 8:30 AM ET (consensus +0.2% MoM core, +2.6% YoY per the yesterday afternoon forecast — the Fed's preferred inflation gauge after the Warsh-Fed-pause-with-hawkish-dissent Wednesday). Soft print (≤+0.2% MoM core) re-anchors disinflation and extends the re-rate; hot print (≥+0.3% MoM core) re-pressures 10Y toward 4.70%+ and forces multiple compression. Personal Income/Spending round out the data. The Fed remains in a post-meeting blackout through next week; next public commentary at Jackson Hole Aug 27-29 with Chair Warsh's keynote.

What to Watch at the Open

  1. AMZN +12% vs AAPL −7% — is the AI-winner rotation extending or fading at the bell? The premarket divergence is the cleanest expression of the new tape thesis: companies whose AI capex generates measurable revenue growth are rewarded; companies whose growth is supply-constrained or whose Q3 guide disappoints are penalized. AMZN's AWS +37% YoY is the gold-standard confirmation; AAPL's 9-11% Q3 guide on memory supply is the gold-standard denial. If AMZN holds above $260 into the cash open, the AI-capex-durable trade extends to GOOGL, NVDA, and the broader chip complex. If AMZN fades back below $250 by 10:00 AM ET, the rotation loses momentum. Watch NVDA premarket tape as the read-through.
  1. June PCE 8:30 AM ET — the binary catalyst that determines whether the AMZN re-rate extends or fades. Consensus +0.2% MoM core, +2.6% YoY per the yesterday afternoon forecast. The Fed's preferred gauge after the Warsh-Fed-pause-with-hawkish-dissent Wednesday (10Y settled at 4.663% Thu close per ^TNX). A soft print (≤+0.2% MoM core) re-anchors the disinflation thesis — AMZN + MSFT + META's three-print validation sequence plus a soft PCE = the cleanest setup for the 7,500 retest. A hot print (≥+0.3% MoM core) forces a 10Y re-test toward 4.70%+ and pressures multiples.
  1. VIX at 16.97 — is the post-FOMC vol normalization durable into the close? VIX dropped from 20.66 (Wed post-FOMC spike) to 17.09 (Thu) to 16.97 (Fri pre) — a -17.9% drawdown in 48 hours. A VIX hold below 17.50 into the close would re-anchor the pre-FOMC vol regime; a re-spike above 18.50 on a hot PCE would confirm the post-FOMC regime remains live. AMZN + MSFT + META + soft PCE = VIX < 17.00; AMZN + MSFT + META + hot PCE = VIX re-tests 18-19 area.

The Structural Read

The AMZN +12% / AAPL −7% premarket divergence is the cleanest expression of the current tape regime: the market is sorting AI-related businesses into three buckets — (1) AI-spend-to-profit translators (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL — rewarded on confirmation); (2) AI-spend-as-headwind (META — punished on capex concerns); (3) AI-adjacent but supply-constrained (AAPL — punished on guidance despite no reported miss). The structural bull case is that bucket (1) is broadening; the bear case is that it's concentrated in 3-4 names. The 10Y at 4.663% suggests the bond market is comfortable with both readings — not pricing recession, not pricing reacceleration.

The post-FOMC reversion (VIX 20.66 → 16.97, SPX 7,316.15 → 7,437.63) is complete in two sessions — a structural signal: the Warsh Fed's 9-3 hawkish-dissent hold was fully absorbed by Thursday's close, and the AMZN-led AI-capex re-rating is the dominant tape driver. The path of least resistance is consolidation in the 7,400-7,500 range as the market digests AMZN's $220B capex raise. The 1-month SPX target of 7,500 is +0.84% above Thursday's 7,437.63 — the closest reading to target since June 12. Friday's close determines whether that target is hit today or deferred into next week's Q2 mega-cap earnings cycle.

Bottom line: AMZN's overnight +12% is the structural event of the week — AWS +37% YoY (fastest since 2021) validates the AI-capex thesis Wednesday's FOMC briefly threatened; AAPL's −7% is the counterpoint. Pre-market futures reflect the AMZN re-anchor (ES +0.36%, NQ +1.13%, RTY +0.39%) and VIX at 16.97 confirms vol normalization. June PCE at 8:30 AM ET is the binary catalyst — soft extends re-rate to 7,500 retest, hot forces 10Y toward 4.70%+. The structural uptrend remains intact; path of least resistance is consolidation in the 7,400-7,500 range pending PCE, then 7,500+ into next week's earnings cycle.
