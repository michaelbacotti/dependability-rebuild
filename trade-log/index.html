Public Trade Log

Every position Dependability Holdings LLC holds in our own portfolio — and every closed trade we have published — is recorded here. Each entry includes the underlying, structure, strike(s), expiration, entry credit or debit, current mark, and the date the position was opened. We update this page when trades open, when we take action (rolls, adjustments, partial closes), and on close.

Why a public trade log? Our methodology page describes what we publish and how we run our portfolio. The trade log is the operational proof — the daily record that backs up every research note, strategy guide, and forecast. Anyone can audit our positions at any time and verify what we hold, what we have closed, and how we have acted.

Disclosure standards

  • Pre-publication rule: we do not publish a directional view on an instrument we currently hold. Any strategy guide that walks through a structure we are simultaneously trading in our own portfolio carries an inline disclosure at the top of the article and a parallel entry in this trade log.
  • Same-day disclosure: when a published forecast opens a trade, that trade is added to this log the same day before market close.
  • Adjustment disclosure: any roll, partial close, or adjustment is logged with timestamp and rationale.
  • Close-out disclosure: closed positions are moved to the closed-trades section with exit mark and P/L.
