Q2 GDP advance estimate (Friday July 31) and July PCE (Friday July 31) are the next two hard-data inputs into the FOMC's rate-path framing.

Four scenarios could push the S&P back toward the bear case of 7,000-7,200: (1) Iran escalation resuming this week — the second-night-pause is not a ceasefire; a resumption of US strikes or an Iranian response to prior strikes would push Brent back above $100 and re-introduce the inflation-fear narrative; (2) Hawkish Powell press conference Wednesday 2:30 PM ET — if Powell uses the elevated oil tape (WTI still in $84 area, Brent $90+) to reinforce "higher for longer" framing and fails to acknowledge the durable goods +0.3% headline as Q3 capex moderation, the market reprices a more hawkish rate path and the 10Y re-tests 4.80-4.90%; (3) META Q2 earnings disappointment Wednesday after-close — META consensus $7.20 EPS on $60.21B revenue; any guidance cut on AI capex or advertising revenue durability would pressure XLK and QQQ; (4) July PCE hot print Friday (+0.3%+ MoM core) — combined with Brent at $90+ and Q2 GDP +2.5%+, a hot PCE gives the Fed cover to signal no cuts in 2026.

On the upside, three scenarios could accelerate the path to new highs: (1) Iran ceasefire progress — any confirmed back-channel diplomatic progress (China facilitating, Pakistan brokering, Switzerland mediating) would push Brent below $85 and re-expand the equity multiple; (2) Dovish Powell Wednesday — a Powell press conference that reads the durable goods +0.3% headline as Q3 moderation and frames oil as supply-side would confirm the "one cut September" path and support the 7,500+ re-test; (3) META and AMZN strong Q2 prints — META confirming AI advertising ramp + capex raise; AMZN confirming AWS growth acceleration + another capex raise to $130B+; the structural AI capex thesis reasserts and the S&P re-activates the 7,600+ path.

For long-term investors, the framework is: stay allocated, view the Iran pause extension and the durable goods +0.3% headline as tactical entry opportunities for quality cyclicals and AI infrastructure, and use the FOMC/META/AMZN week as the next data checkpoint for the 3-12 month thesis. The structural uptrend remains intact. The Q2 bank earnings validated the financial-sector thesis. The TSMC capex raise validated the AI infrastructure thesis. The Iran pause is a diplomatic opening — not a resolution — but it is the most significant de-escalation signal in six weeks. The smart positioning is to be long the structural themes (AI infrastructure, financials, US growth) and use this week's volatility as the entry window for high-quality names, with the FOMC Wednesday 2:00 PM ET, META Q2 after-close Wednesday, and AMZN Q2 Thursday after-close as the catalysts that determine the next directional move.
