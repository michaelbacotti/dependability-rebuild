Daily Move: Monday, August 10 — Record Close Holds, Defensive Rotation Leads

As of Monday, August 10, 2026 (4:00 PM ET close), the S&P 500 closed at 7,753.11 — essentially flat at -0.06% on the day versus Friday August 7's record close of 7,757.64 and +2.01% WTD from Monday August 3's 7,600.50. The session was a textbook post-record-close positioning refresh: the index opened near Friday's close, traded in a tight ~30-point intraday range (the day's high was approximately 7,766, the low approximately 7,735), and closed just below Friday's record. The VIX held at 14.90 Friday close (yfinance — public.com does not provide VIX quotes) — a level last seen before the prior week's payrolls catalyst — confirming the market is treating the 7,757.64 print as support rather than resistance. Oil (WTI via yfinance) closed Friday at $78.18, up modestly on the week. The 10-year yield held at 4.66% (yfinance Friday close) — unchanged on the day per yfinance data. The day's narrative: defensive rotation led (XLE +4.66% on oil bid, XLV +1.76%, GLD +1.06%) while tech gave back (XLK -0.69%, QQQ -0.24%) — a textbook post-rally consolidation pattern that leaves the structural uptrend intact. The 1-month target of 7,800 is +0.60% above current and within single-session reach on a soft Wednesday CPI print. The 3-month target of 7,900 is +1.89% above current and the year-end 2026 base case of 8,000 is +3.18% above current — both pending Wednesday's July CPI binary test (consensus: headline -0.4% MoM, core +0.2% MoM YoY +2.6%).
